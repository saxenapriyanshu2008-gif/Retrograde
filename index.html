  1. As found

    A 1962 saloon, pulled out of a scrapyard in Kanpur. The body is straight. The engine is finished.

  2. Taken apart

    Roof, bonnet, boot, doors and wheels come off. We keep every panel and the shape they make.

  3. The old heart

    A 1.5 litre petrol engine, a four-speed gearbox, a 40 litre tank and a rusted exhaust. About 240 kg of iron.

  4. Out it goes

    Engine, gearbox, tank and exhaust all come out. The iron is sent for recycling, not to a landfill.

  5. The new heart

    A 52 kWh battery goes flat under the floor, so the cabin keeps its space. A 110 kW motor drives the rear wheels.

  6. Modern touches

    LED ring headlamps, a light bar at the back, aero wheels and a charging port hidden behind the old fuel flap.

  7. Rebuilt

    Every panel goes back where it came from. Old shape, new heart. Car number 001 is ready.

It started in a scrapyard.

In Delhi, a petrol car older than 15 years cannot stay on the road. Every year, thousands of old cars are towed away and crushed. Many of them still have their chrome, their round headlamps and their stories.

Walking through one of those yards, we saw a 1962 saloon under years of dust. The body was fine. Only the engine was finished.

So we asked a simple question. What if we kept the shape everyone loves and gave it a heart that is clean, quiet and legal for decades more?

Retrograde is that answer. We rescue shells from scrapyards across India and rebuild them one at a time. Old shape, new heart.
