Inside the Ghati 01

  1. The Ghati 01

    A 40 mm automatic. 186 parts, put together by hand. Keep scrolling and we take it apart.

  2. Opened up

    Crystal, bezel, hands, dial, movement, rotor and caseback. Every layer lifts away in the order a watchmaker would take it apart.

  3. Sapphire crystal

    Rated 9 on the Mohs scale, so only a diamond can scratch it. Anti-reflective coating on both sides.

  4. Sunburst dial

    Brushed from the centre out so light runs across it as you turn your wrist. Indices are applied one at a time.

  5. The balance wheel

    The heartbeat. It swings 28,800 times an hour, eight beats a second, on a hairspring thinner than a hair.

  6. Mainspring and gears

    One full wind stores 72 hours of power. Four wheels pass it on, slowed down to exactly one turn an hour.

  7. The rotor

    A weighted half-moon that spins as your arm moves and winds the spring for you. You never need to wind it.

  8. Back together

    Every part returns to its place. Through the caseback you can watch it all keep time.

Kalpa. Watches made to outlast us.

Each Kalpa takes between four and fourteen months to make. Dials use crafts that took centuries to learn. Only a few are made each year, and every one is signed by the person who made it.

  • Kalpa Bidri

    A dial in Bidriware from Bidar, Karnataka. Pure silver wire is hammered into blackened zinc alloy by hand.

    Made this year
    12 pieces
    Time to make
    5 months

  • Kalpa Ulka

    A dial cut from an iron meteorite. Acid etching brings out the crystal pattern that formed in space as it cooled.

    Made this year
    8 pieces
    Time to make
    4 months

  • Kalpa Jantar

    Shows the moon phase and the tithi, the lunar day of the Indian calendar. Inspired by the Jantar Mantar observatories.
